# Listing text - AKForeverAltMailer

**Summary (one line):** Your alts get what they use: at any mailbox, herbs to your alchemist, cloth to your tailor, BoE gear to your enchanter or the alt it is an upgrade for, class supplies to that class, valuables to your banker - previewed, then one button.

## Description

**Alt Mailer** - for WoW: Forever.

Log in on each of your alts once. From then on, open any mailbox and a panel beside the mail window shows
what in your bags belongs with which character - and why - and one button mails it.

- **Profession materials to the alt highest in the profession.** Herbs to your alchemist, cloth to your
  tailor, ore and bars to your blacksmith, leather to your leatherworker, dust and essences to your enchanter,
  meat and fish to your cook, parts to your engineer. When you are that alt, they stay with you.
- **Gear to whoever wears it best.** Green-or-better gear that binds when worn goes to the alt it is the biggest
  upgrade for - the right armor and weapon types, near its level, stats that suit its class. Nobody? To your
  enchanter, to disenchant.
- **Class supplies to that class.** Candles, powders, runes, symbols, ankhs, soul shards, your enchanter's wizard
  and mana oils for your casters and healers; arrows and bullets to your hunter. What you use yourself stays.
- **A banker for the rest.** Pick one (`/aam banker <name>`) and tradeable valuables no other alt takes go there:
  greens and better, recipes, anything a vendor pays a gold for. Your potions, food and reagents stay with you.
- **You decide.** The Receivers list picks another alt for any profession; a right-click on an item keeps it for
  good. Soulbound things, quest items, keys, greys and the hearthstone always stay.
- **Mails of twelve**, the goods named in the subject, the postage on the button.

## Commands

`/aam` (or `/altmail`):

- `/aam plan` - what would go, and to whom, from anywhere
- `/aam receivers`, `/aam banker <name>`, `/aam alts`, `/aam forget <name>`
- `/aam unkeep` - your keep list
- `/aam set` - the options
- `/aam show`, `/aam send`, `/aam diag`
